The Bespoke Journey

From Concept to Creation

Creating a custom piece of jewellery is an intimate and collaborative experience. At RUVA, we guide you through every step of this journey, ensuring the final creation is as unique as the story it represents.

1

Discovery

We begin by understanding you. In our first consultation, we discuss your outfit, personality, occasion, and timeline. This establishes the foundational constraints and inspiration for the design.

2

Design & Concepts

Our studio translates your vision into detailed sketches and design concepts. We explore proportions and material balances, refining through revisions until the piece perfectly reflects your intention.

3

Meticulous Craft

Upon design approval, we source the precise diamonds and gold required. Our master goldsmiths begin the careful work of bringing the piece to life, ensuring exceptional quality control at every stage.

4

Finalisation

The completed piece undergoes a final fitting to ensure exquisite comfort and scale. Once finished to perfection, we beautifully package and deliver your commission.

Typical Timeline Expectation: 3 to 6 Weeks

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does custom bridal jewellery take?
On average, a bespoke piece requires between 3 to 6 weeks from the initial discovery consultation to the final delivery. This allows us the necessary time to refine designs and execute the craftsmanship to our stringent standards.
Can I customise diamond shapes?
Absolutely. We work intimately with trusted diamantaires to source specific cuts, clarities, and colors that align seamlessly with your finalized design.
Do you work outside weddings?
While our expertise is deeply rooted in bridal creations, we regularly commission bespoke pieces for anniversaries, milestone achievements, and personal heritage collections. The journey remains the same.
How is pricing structured?
Because every piece is entirely bespoke, pricing varies significantly based on material complexity, diamond selection, and design intricacy. After our Discovery phase, we provide a structured transparent proposal for your approval.
